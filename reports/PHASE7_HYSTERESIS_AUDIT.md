# Phase 7: existing edge-linking semantics (pre-implementation audit)

Workspace was clean at `b5a3280`; `rtl/baseline`, `rtl/phase3`, `rtl/phase4`, `rtl/phase4b`, `rtl/phase5b`, and `rtl/phase6` are protected and unchanged.

The Phase 6 top instantiates `canny_doubleThreshold_phase5b` (`rtl/phase6/canny_edge_detect_phase6_top.v:61-65`). In that module, `max_g` is a 2-bit class, and a 3×3 `matrix_generate_3x3_phase5b` produces nine class taps (`rtl/phase5b/canny_doubleThreshold_phase5b.v:19-61`). `search` ORs bit 1 of **all nine taps**, including the center (`:65-66`). `high_low` ORs the two center bits (`:67`). The registered result is `high_low && search` (`:87-94`). Thus a strong center retains itself; a weak center is retained only when at least one immediately adjacent strong class exists; class zero never survives. There is no promoted-edge feedback or iteration: this is **one-pass local weak-edge promotion**, not recursive Canny hysteresis.

The window has two line-delay RAMs (`rtl/phase5b/matrix_generate_3x3_phase5b.v`, `rtl/baseline/one_column_ram.v`, `rtl/baseline/fifo_ram.v`). Horizontal taps clear on inactive delayed href; Phase 5B row-valid state substitutes zero for unavailable previous-frame rows (`rtl/phase5b/matrix_generate_3x3_phase5b.v`). The output controls are delayed to match the registered result (`rtl/phase5b/canny_doubleThreshold_phase5b.v:70-99`). The line-buffer window is causal; its output sample coordinate is **not** necessarily the geometric coordinate of its center class. A direct spatial comparison of the production output image to an unshifted software flood fill would conflate this transport alignment with hysteresis semantics. Phase 7 therefore compares the two connectivity rules on the **same post-NMS classification map in class-map coordinates**. Historic cycle-exact RTL/Python equivalence for the production window remains the Phase 6 regression (`reports/PHASE6_RUNTIME_THRESHOLDS.md`); no RTL changes are made here.
