# Phase 9 stripe schedule audit (before RTL validation)

The tested Phase 8 source cadence is 640 active + 24 blank clocks per line. A 32×32 stripe collects 32×664=21,248 clocks and replays 32×640+31=20,511 clocks. A full 64×64 stripe collects 64×664=42,496 clocks and replays 64×640+63=41,023 clocks. The last 64-wide stripe contains only 32 **real** image rows: collection 21,248 and replay 20,511 clocks. No padded pixels enter its histogram or output.

`scripts/phase9/schedule_analysis.py` models ordered threshold scans (one cycle/bin, launch and drain, followed by one cycle/address clear), ordered raster replay, and bank reuse. Without relying on vertical blanking, two banks are safe for all tested 32-row configurations. Two banks **fail** for every 64-row configuration at stripe 8, the first stripe of the next frame: the bank containing stripe 6 is still in replay. Deficit: 19,857 clocks (8 bins), 19,937 (16), 20,097 (32). Three banks are analytically safe over three uninterrupted frames. This is an analytical schedule check; an RTL collision assertion and full-frame simulation must still pass.

Storage before physical mapping: 32-row bank=20,480×11=225,280 bits; two banks=450,560 bits. 64-row bank=40,960×11=450,560 bits; three banks=1,351,680 bits. The 64-row third bank is a **correctness requirement** at the tested no-backpressure cadence, not a silent optimization. The Phase 8 synthesized mapping used 11 RAMB36 per 32-row bank; a linear depth estimate is 22 RAMB36 per 64-row bank, or 66 for three banks. Actual mapping must be measured by Phase 9 synthesis. Histogram counters are 11 bits for 32×32 and 13 bits for 64×64. There are 20 blocks per 32-row stripe and 10 per 64-row stripe; 64-row final-stripe blocks contain 2,048 real pixels, not 4,096.

XC7A100T reports 135 RAMB36 tiles (270 RAMB18 equivalents). If the 66-RAMB36 estimate holds, a shared front-end using the Phase 8 equivalent of four additional RAMB36 tiles would make two 64-row adaptive engines exceed 135 tiles (2×66+4=136) before other storage. Thus 64-row blocks may be unsuitable for Phase 10 replication even if one engine fits. This is an architectural estimate, not a synthesized Phase 9 result.
